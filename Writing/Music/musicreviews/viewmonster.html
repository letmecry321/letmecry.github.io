view monster - lemon demon

reviewed : h

View Monster is Lemon Demon's 9th(?) album, released in 2008. I believe this to be his magnum opus, and a far superior work to his later album "Spirit Phone". The album is unique in that it has 16 tracks, but after each is an untitled track that Neil calls a "palatte cleanser". I will be including my ratings of these in the review based on pretty much how well they fit and serve their purpose. Neil has two seperate commentaries on the album whcih i would recommend reading if youre interested.
